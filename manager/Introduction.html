Thank you for agreeing to participate in this study.

You will receive a £2 participation fee for completing the study, which will take approximately 20 minutes.

In this study, you will take the role of a Manager. Your task is to evaluate {{ total_decisions }} pairs of workers who completed a previous experiment.

Each pair consists of two Prolific participants who completed a letter-ordering work task.

As the Manager, your job is to decide how to divide £1 (100 pence) between the two workers in each pair.

You can divide the £1 however you like. For example:

  • 50p to each worker;
  • 80p to one worker and 20p to the other; or
  • £1 to one worker and £0 to the other.

There is no right or wrong way to divide the money. We are interested in how people choose to divide rewards based on the information they observe.
